# Design Analysis: ACA Hydraulic

## Overview
The user wants a replica of a mobile website for "ACA Hydraulic". The design is industrial, dark, and focused on heavy machinery service.

## Visual Style
- **Theme:** Dark, Industrial, Rugged.
- **Color Palette:**
  - **Primary Background:** Dark Grey / Black / Dark Stone Texture.
  - **Accent Color:** Industrial Yellow/Orange (approx. `#FFC107` or `#E6B800`). Used for buttons, icons, and highlights.
  - **Text Color:** White (Headlines), Light Grey (Body text).
- **Typography:** Strong, condensed sans-serif for headings (likely Oswald, Roboto Condensed, or similar). Clean sans-serif for body text (Roboto, Inter).
- **Imagery:** High-quality, dramatic photography of heavy machinery (excavators) in working environments (mines, quarries). Dark, moody lighting with high contrast.

## Key Sections & Content

### 1. Header
- **Logo:** "ACA HYDRAULIC" (Yellow square icon + White text).
- **Contact:** Phone number `+7 771 417 79 25` (Yellow).

- **Background:** Full-screen image of a yellow excavator in a rocky/mountainous mining environment. Dark overlay/vignette to ensure text readability.
- **Headline:** "ВЫЕЗДНОЙ СЕРВИС ГИДРАВЛИКИ СПЕЦТЕХНИКИ" (Uppercase, Bold, White).
- **Subheadline:** "Диагностика и ремонт гидросистем экскаваторов и буровых установок на объекте заказчика." (Light grey).
- **CTA Buttons:**

  - Secondary: "ПОЛУЧИТЬ КОНСУЛЬТАЦИЮ" (Transparent background, White border, White text).

### 3. "Why Trust Us" Section (Benefits)

### 4. Brands Ticker/Row

### 5. Sticky Footer / Action Bar
- **Button 1:** "ПОЗВОНИТЬ" (Yellow background, Black text, Phone icon).

## Functional Requirements
- **Responsive Design:** Must look exactly like the screenshot on mobile, and adapt gracefully to desktop (likely keeping the hero section prominent).
- **Interactivity:** Buttons should have hover states. "Call" and "WhatsApp" buttons should function as `tel:` and `wa.me` links.
