# K7V280DTP product review

Draft catalogue addition. Merge and live publication require separate approval.

## Identity and photographs

The requested product designation is **K7V280DTP**. The four supplied photographs show a yellow two-section pump from the side, drive-shaft/flange end, rear end and above. A readable model/manufacturer nameplate is not present. A cast housing mark is not an OEM part number or sufficient model identification.

The original JPEG files are retained without retouching, cropping or generated imagery. Their byte hashes are checked in `tests/k7v280dtp-product.test.mjs`.

No exact K7V280/K7V280DTP product or pull request was found in the repository before this addition. The new canonical handle is `k7v280dtp-hydraulic-pump`.

## Fitment evidence

- [Kawasaki K7V family](https://global.kawasaki.com/en/industrial_equipment/hydraulic/pumps/k7v.html) describes the pump family and tandem K7V280. This does not identify the photographed item or establish its manufacturer, exact suffix, pressure or displacement.
- [Cat 566-8632](https://parts.cat.com/en/catcorp/566-8632) lists 349, 352 and 352 UHD, but does not establish K7V280DTP as an interchangeable designation.
- Cat also lists distinct main-pump references for [374: 627-1302](https://parts.cat.com/en/catcorp/product/627-1302) and [395: 627-1304](https://parts.cat.com/en/catcorp/product/627-1304).

These sources do **not** confirm one K7V280DTP configuration fits all four requested CAT models. The public card therefore treats CAT 349, 352, 374 and 395 as individual selection inquiries, not a confirmed interchange list. No OEM numbers from this research are assigned to the product.

## Commercial boundary

Price and availability are on request. The card makes no warehouse-stock, original Kawasaki/Cat, country-of-origin, new/rebuilt condition, test, warranty, fixed delivery-date or technical-rating claim. Final selection needs the complete pump nameplate/code, OEM number, machine serial number, shaft, flange, ports, regulator and connectors.

The card uses the existing owner-product preparation pipeline and local product-scoped images. After combining this branch with another catalogue addition, run `node scripts/prepare-public-catalog.mjs` to regenerate the shared snapshots rather than manually merging the minified JSON files.
